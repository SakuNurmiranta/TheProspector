## DESIGN REFERENCE: Agents (Generic)

**Examples of agent interactions:**
- Collective action: You decide to use the first action (for your band) for rehearsals and the second for recording. You won't push it.
- Check-action: After last season's rehearsal stint you notice that your drummer (band member) is under performing compared to others. His musician skill has a hidden, negative modifier. You ask their partner (groupie) what is wrong. The action (diagnose) is not significant and can be handled locally (client side, this is not a complete action, but a "check" action).
  - The mechanics determine that groupie agent wants their owner's negative traits removed, so the check succeeds. It turns out that your drummer's side project has been confusing them and tapping into their resources.
- Regular vs Band  member action: In your band member's backlog,  a grind core project with some third party is revealed. You choose "leave project: grind core" as your drummers first action. You check for Regular vs Agent. This action goes to agent bucket.
- Regular vs Band member action: You tell your drummer to take it easy for the rest of the season (rest action).
- At resolution phase
  - The band member participates in the collective actions (because they are unopposed)
  - The groupie's attachment trait is altered by the interaction towards their owner and their owners' owner.
  - The test to see if the band member quits the side project fails on the Host. He continues it in secret (reflected on the backlog).
  - The band member skips action and removes a negative trait.
