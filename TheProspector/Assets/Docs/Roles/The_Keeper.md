## DESIGN REFERENCE: The Keeper

#### *blurring the lines between administrative and player game roles*

The host of the game is called The Keeper. After each season (4 turns), their authority is challenged by the other players via the game mechanics. If the Keeper changes,
the status of Host also migrates to another player. The Keeper has asymmetric advantages over the other players:
1) They can lose other player's tapes in the mail (these demos can be retrieved by somebody else later, should the become the Keeper)
2) replace a vote with a blank note,
3) they have authority over the Posers. 
4) They also have access to the session menu, which might be used to their advantage. 

They also have the game mechanical benefit of free access and complete control over:
1) an album **retailer**
2) music **promotion**
3) an important **bulletin board** 
4) **a socialization hub**

Here is the catch: While being the Keeper, the player doesn't have the time to work on their project. This means that their band cannot release new material. 

In order to win the game, a player must be the last Keeper and out maneuver the last rival band away from the scene. 
The game can only be won by scheming, as true artistic output plays second fiddle as the victory condition.
