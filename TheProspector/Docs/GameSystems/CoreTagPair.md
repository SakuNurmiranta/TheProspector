## CoreTagPair Mechanic

***in search of genre definition mechanics***

In order for something to be within the defined borders of acceptence (TRV) those borders must be set in stone at the most rudimentary level. TRVtags are "alive" and define the overall thematic sound of a certain genre, but what is required is the very **essence** of the genre - which is defined by how CoreTagPairs work. Black metal, which is at the core of this game, has a CoreTagPair of re-sacralising logic, ie. something that is socially a taboo (f.ex satanism, solipsism, detachment from humanity) replaces the normative choice that is its polar opposite (f.ex christianity, inclusivity, humanism). The CoreTagPair defines black metal. Other genres have their own approach to the central tags, but that mostly falls outside the scope of this analysis.

Examples of CoreTagPair are prevalent throughout the lyrical and sonic themes of black metal: 
In Bathory's "Woman of Dark Desires", the much revered bloody countess (Elisabeth Bathory) is praised as an anti-mother who takes lives in order to stay young forever; 
In the whole design aesthetic of Dark Throne's 90s discography (and probably beyond), the ease of access (of warmth, of professional and "nice" production) is spat upon and replaced with harshness that is viewed as a sign of genuine action and honesty; 
Burzum's catalogue expresses uneasiness towards black metal tropes themselves, occasionally forcing simple and mellow, digital CASIO-soundscapes to the listeners who are expecting fast and brutal expression with regular band instruments; Immortal's fetishization of a cold hell scape fantasy world Blashyrkh is yet another example of replacing good with bad.

The CoreTagPair creates an important distinction between "black metal" and "true black metal". A band can succeed as a black metal band without meeting the true black metal criteria, simply by following black metal tropes and aspects without having a firm or "true" core at its center. In the game, this situation would stand out as a case where the band has all the TRV tags and aspects to make it qualify as black metal, but lacking the CoreTagPair dynamic. At the opposite side of the spectrum, a band that fails to have the correct TRV tags and aspects, but has the CoreTagPair dynamic intact can be considered "blackened" other genre.
