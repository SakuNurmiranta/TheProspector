## DESIGN REFERENCE: The Keeper

#### *blurring the lines between administrative and player game roles*

The host of the game is called The Keeper. After each season (4 turns), their authority is challenged by the other players via the game mechanics. If the Keeper changes,
the status of Host also migrates to another player. The Keeper has asymmetric advantages over the other players:
1) They can lose other player's tapes in the mail (these demos can be retrieved by somebody else later, should the become the Keeper)
2) replace a vote with a blank note,
3) they have authority over the Posers. 
4) they can turn a Regular member of the Circle into a Pariah.
5) they receive a percentage of **clout** from the Circle releases.

They also have the game mechanical benefit of free access and complete control over:
1) an album **retailer**
2) music **promotion**
3) an important **bulletin board** 
4) **a socialization hub**

Here is the catch: While being the Keeper, the player doesn't have the time to work on their project. This means that their band cannot release new material. 

In order to win the game, a player must be the last Keeper and out maneuver the last rival band away from the scene. 
The game can only be won by scheming, as true artistic output plays second fiddle as the victory condition.

A Regular Player becomes the Keeper by having their band release an album that alters gently the sound of True, and then challenging the old Keeper with their clout.
A retaliatory challenge can be issued when the current Keeper is making a Keeper action (declare Pariah) against a Regular player while suffering from certain negative effects of past failed Keeper action.
The one with more clout wins the challenge.

### The First Keeper
The Player who is randomly selected to be the first Keeper has produced a record that is considered to be **First Wave**, and receives clout from all the subsequent releases (The whole of Second Wave/the whole game). The balancing act is that nothing that original record contains is considered "pure", because it doesn't belong to this decade, so it has diminishing returns from year to year. And because the First Keeper has no access to new material from their band, they will be ousted fast.

### Cursed Tapes and the Trash Bin
The tapes that each Keeper decides to ignore are never removed from play, but pile up into Keeper's trash bin first. The trash bin is emptied every other year, so if there is a change of Keeper, the last years ignored tapes are still inside the bin. 
The current Keeper can access the that were discarded last year and retrieve them. After a year has passed, the tapes become beyond their reach.
They can be accessed (listened) in counter chronological order, but the whole heap can be checked (read) freely by the Current Keeper. A demo is considered usable once it has been listened.
