## DESIGN REFERENCE: THE REGULAR

The player who participates in the game is called "The Regular", as long as they are not hosting. If they become hosts via migration for any reason, they are referred as The Keeper.

The Regular is a band leader, who is vying to keep their project "True" within the social hierarchy of "The Circle", while simultaneously trying to stay true to their own vision.
A Regular must manage the band (rehearsals, gigs, recording sessions, resources etc...) and make the ever important estimations on the direction of the sound and the band in general. As long as the Regular belongs to the Circle, they are in a sense working for the Keeper, building over the Keeper's legacy, in exchange of cheaper services and a modicum of safety from being cut down as an artist. 

A special state for Regular is that of The Pariah. Usually, a Pariah is born when the Keeper or other members of the Circle sees another band leader as a threat to their legacy and decides to cast them out. 

The Pariah has access to Garbage Can. This is where the Keeper's trashes end up. Accessing a certain tape requires the entire heap to be resolved up to the moment back in time the tape ended to the Garbage can.
